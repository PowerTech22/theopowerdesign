About

Designer, developer, and the person who answers the email.

I'm Theo. I build websites and apps, and wire up the automation behind them for businesses that would rather be doing their actual work.

I started building sites for small businesses because I kept meeting owners who had paid for something they couldn't edit, couldn't measure and didn't really like. The site looked fine. It just wasn't doing a job.

Since then the work has grown into two connected things: the site and the systems behind it. Keeping both under one roof means fewer meetings, fewer suppliers, and nothing falling through the gaps between them.

I work with a small number of clients at a time. That's deliberate. It's the only way to stay hands-on from the first call through launch — and to still be useful a month later.

You'll always know what's happening, what it costs, and what happens next.

How I work

Three principles.

01

Clarity over cleverness

If a visitor can't tell what you do and what to do next within five seconds, no amount of styling will save it. Structure first, decoration later.

02

Built to be handed over

Everything I deliver is documented and editable by you. You should never need me to change a price or swap a photo.

03

Measure what matters

Enquiries, bookings, hours saved. I'd rather report three numbers that change decisions than twenty that look busy.
